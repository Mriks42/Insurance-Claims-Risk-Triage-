# Claims Triage and Investigation Procedures

## Section 1: Triage Decision Framework

### 1.1 Three-Tier Triage System
All incoming claims shall be classified into one of three triage buckets based on their computed risk score:

- **SIU Escalation (Top 5%)**: Claims in the highest risk tier must be referred to the Special Investigations Unit. These claims require a full field investigation including interviews, site inspections, and cross-referencing with industry fraud databases. SIU-escalated claims should not be paid until the investigation is complete.

- **Manual Review (Next 15%)**: Claims in the moderate risk tier require manual reviewer assessment. The reviewer must verify all documentation, contact the policyholder for clarification, and confirm claim details against the police report. Manual review claims may be approved, denied, or escalated to SIU based on the reviewer's findings.

- **Approve (Remaining 80%)**: Claims in the lowest risk tier may be processed through standard automated approval workflows. Random sampling of 2% of approved claims should be conducted quarterly to validate triage accuracy.

### 1.2 Risk Score Interpretation
A risk score above 0.50 indicates a strong probability of fraud and should always trigger SIU escalation regardless of the automated triage bucket assignment. Risk scores between 0.30 and 0.50 require manual review at minimum. Risk scores below 0.10 are considered low-risk and suitable for expedited processing.

### 1.3 Reason Code Requirements
Every claim routed to SIU or Manual Review must include machine-generated reason codes explaining the primary risk factors. Reason codes must identify the top 3-5 features driving the risk score and express them in plain language that non-technical investigators can understand.

## Section 2: SIU Investigation Protocols

### 2.1 Investigation Triggers
A claim must be referred to SIU when any of the following conditions are met:
- Risk score exceeds the SIU threshold (top 5%)
- Multiple fraud indicators are present simultaneously (3 or more red flags)
- The policyholder has a prior fraud finding on record
- The claim involves a recently changed address combined with no police report
- The claim involves a high-value vehicle with liability-only coverage

### 2.2 Investigation Procedures
SIU investigators must:
1. Review the complete claim file and all supporting documentation
2. Conduct recorded interviews with the policyholder and any witnesses
3. Perform vehicle inspection or arrange for independent appraisal
4. Cross-reference the claim against the National Insurance Crime Bureau (NICB) database
5. Document all findings in a formal investigation report
6. Make a determination within 30 business days

### 2.3 Documentation Standards
All investigation findings must be documented in the standardized SIU Report Template. Reports must include a summary of findings, evidence collected, interview transcripts, and a final determination with supporting rationale. Denied claims must include specific policy provisions that support the denial.

## Section 3: Manual Review Procedures

### 3.1 Reviewer Responsibilities
Manual reviewers must:
1. Verify that the police report (if filed) matches the claim details
2. Confirm the policyholder's identity and coverage status
3. Validate repair estimates against industry benchmarks
4. Review the machine-generated reason codes and assess their relevance
5. Document the review decision and supporting rationale

### 3.2 Escalation Criteria
During manual review, a claim should be escalated to SIU if:
- The reviewer identifies discrepancies between the claim and supporting documents
- The policyholder is uncooperative or provides inconsistent statements
- Additional red flags are discovered that were not captured by the automated system
- The claim amount exceeds policy limits or appears inflated

### 3.3 Review Timeline
Manual reviews must be completed within 10 business days. If additional information is required from the policyholder, a 15-day extension may be granted. Claims pending beyond 25 days must be escalated to a senior reviewer.
